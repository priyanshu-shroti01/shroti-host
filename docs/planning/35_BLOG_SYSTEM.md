# 35_BLOG_SYSTEM

**Project:** ShrotiHost
**Document Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Blog System for ShrotiHost. The blog serves as a platform for educational content, product updates, tutorials, announcements, and SEO-driven articles that help users succeed with web hosting.

---

# Primary Goals

- Educate customers
- Improve organic search traffic
- Build trust and authority
- Support customer onboarding
- Showcase product updates

---

# Target Audience

- Developers
- Students
- Bloggers
- Freelancers
- Small Businesses
- Startup Founders

---

# Blog Structure

1. Blog Homepage
2. Categories
3. Tags
4. Featured Articles
5. Individual Article Page
6. Author Profile
7. Related Articles
8. Newsletter Signup

---

# Homepage

Display:

- Featured article
- Latest posts
- Popular posts
- Categories
- Search bar

---

# Categories

Suggested categories:

- Hosting
- WordPress
- Domains
- Security
- Tutorials
- Business
- Product Updates
- Company News

---

# Article Layout

Each article should include:

- Title
- Featured image
- Author
- Publication date
- Reading time
- Table of contents
- Main content
- Related articles
- Comments (future)

---

# Search & Filtering

Support:

- Keyword search
- Category filter
- Tag filter
- Sort by newest or popular

---

# Author Profiles

Include:

- Name
- Bio
- Social links
- Published articles

---

# Newsletter

Allow visitors to subscribe for:

- Product updates
- Tutorials
- Promotions
- Company announcements

---

# SEO

Implement:

- Descriptive URLs
- Meta titles
- Meta descriptions
- Open Graph tags
- Structured data
- XML sitemap integration

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Responsive layouts
- WCAG AA compliance

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Page views
- Reading time
- Search usage
- Newsletter signups
- Social shares

---

# Success Metrics

- Organic traffic growth
- Increased article engagement
- Newsletter subscribers
- Reduced support tickets through educational content

---

# Acceptance Checklist

The Blog System should:

- Be easy to navigate
- Support SEO best practices
- Offer fast search
- Encourage content discovery
- Follow the ShrotiHost design system
