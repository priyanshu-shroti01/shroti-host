# Purpose

This document defines the primary customer personas for ShrotiHost. Every feature, page, pricing decision, onboarding flow, and marketing campaign should consider the needs of these users.

---

# Persona 1 — Student

## Profile
- College or university student
- Beginner to intermediate technical knowledge
- Limited budget
- Building portfolios, assignments, hackathon projects, or final-year projects

## Goals
- Launch quickly
- Spend as little as possible
- Learn hosting
- Build a professional portfolio

## Pain Points
- Expensive hosting
- Complicated setup
- Domain costs
- Lack of guidance

## What They Value
- Student discounts
- Free SSL
- Free migration
- Free subdomain
- Simple dashboard
- Helpful documentation

Recommended Plan:
Launch / Student Plan

---

# Persona 2 — Developer

## Profile
- Freelancer or software developer
- Comfortable with cPanel and development tools

## Goals
- Fast deployment
- Reliable performance
- Node.js, Python, Laravel support
- SSH access

## Pain Points
- Slow servers
- Restrictive hosting
- Poor documentation

## What They Value
- Performance
- Modern technology
- Clear documentation
- Developer-friendly support

Recommended Plan:
Grow or Scale

---

# Persona 3 — Startup Founder

## Profile
- Early-stage startup
- Small team
- Focused on launching an MVP

## Goals
- Affordable infrastructure
- Room to grow
- Reliable support

## Pain Points
- Limited budget
- Infrastructure complexity

## What They Value
- Transparent pricing
- Easy upgrades
- Migration support

Recommended Plan:
Grow

# Persona 4 — Freelancer

## Profile
- Builds websites for clients

## Goals
- Reliable hosting
- Easy migration

- Slow support

## What They Value

- Fast migrations
- Easy management

Scale

# Persona 5 — Small Business

## Profile
- Business owner with little technical knowledge

## Goals
- Professional online presence
- Reliable email
- Security

## Pain Points
- Confusing technical terms
- Fear of downtime

## What They Value
- Trust
- Simplicity
- Helpful support

Recommended Plan:
Grow

---

# Shared Expectations

All personas expect:

- Honest pricing
- Fast websites
- Secure hosting
- Helpful documentation
- Responsive support
- Easy onboarding

---

# Design Implications

The website should:

- Explain concepts clearly
- Reduce technical jargon
- Guide users toward suitable plans
- Build confidence before purchase

---

# AI Advisor Considerations

The future Hosting Advisor should identify the visitor's persona by asking simple questions and recommending the most suitable plan.

Example questions:

- What are you building?
- Are you a student?
- Do you need WordPress?
- How many websites do you want to host?
- What is your monthly budget?

---

# Success Criteria

Every persona should be able to:

- Understand the platform
- Find the right hosting plan
- Complete checkout confidently
- Launch without confusion
