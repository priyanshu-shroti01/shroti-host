# Purpose

This document defines every hosting product offered by ShrotiHost, including positioning, target audience, feature philosophy, upgrade paths, and future evolution.

The goal is to create products that are simple to understand while serving different customer needs.

---

# Product Philosophy

Customers should choose a plan based on what they want to build—not on technical specifications alone.

Every product should answer:

- Who is this for?
- Why should they choose it?
- How can they grow?
