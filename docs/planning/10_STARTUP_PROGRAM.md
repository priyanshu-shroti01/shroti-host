# Purpose

The Startup Program is designed to help founders launch products quickly with reliable infrastructure, transparent pricing, and a clear upgrade path.

This initiative focuses on reducing friction during the earliest stages of building a company.

---

# Vision

Help founders spend less time managing hosting and more time building products.

ShrotiHost should become the preferred hosting platform for bootstrapped startups, indie hackers, SaaS founders, and small product teams.

---

# Objectives

- Make launching an MVP simple.
- Keep infrastructure affordable.
- Provide room to scale.
- Reduce technical complexity.
- Build long-term customer relationships.

4. Complete checkout

# AI Hosting Advisor

Questions should include:

- What are you building?
- Expected monthly visitors?
- Preferred technology stack?
- Team size?
- Current hosting provider?

The AI should recommend the most suitable plan with a clear explanation.
