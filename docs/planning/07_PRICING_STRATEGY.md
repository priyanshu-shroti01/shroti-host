# 07_PRICING_STRATEGY

**Project:** ShrotiHost  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines the pricing philosophy, packaging strategy, discount structure, and positioning for all ShrotiHost products.

The objective is not to become the cheapest hosting provider. The objective is to provide the highest value in the affordable hosting segment.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Our pricing must be:

- Affordable
- Transparent
- Sustainable
- Easy to understand
- Competitive
- Honest

Customers should understand what they are paying for without hidden conditions.

---

# Business Position

ShrotiHost competes on:

- Customer experience
- Simplicity
- Performance
- Helpful support
- Modern technology

ShrotiHost does **not** compete by racing to the lowest possible price.

---

# Product Ladder

1. Launch
2. Grow (Recommended)
3. Scale

Every plan should have a clear upgrade path.

---

# Hero Plan

The **Grow** plan is the recommended plan across the website.

Reasons:

- Best balance of price and features.
- Suitable for most customers.
- Strong value proposition.

---

# Billing Cycles

Offer:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semi-Annual
- Annual

Annual plans should provide the greatest savings while maintaining healthy margins.

---

# Student Program

Verified students receive dedicated pricing.

Benefits include:

- Lower pricing
- Free migration
- Portfolio hosting
- Launch subdomain
- Educational resources

Student pricing is a long-term initiative, not a temporary promotion.

---

# Startup Program

Designed for founders launching MVPs.

Possible benefits:

- Introductory discounts
- Easy upgrades
- Migration assistance
- Priority onboarding

---

# Discounts

Acceptable promotions:

- Annual billing savings
- Student pricing
- Startup campaigns
- Festival offers
- Referral rewards

Avoid permanent heavy discounts that reduce brand value.

---

# Renewals

Renewal pricing must be communicated clearly.

Avoid misleading introductory offers followed by excessive renewal increases.

Trust is more valuable than short-term conversions.

---

# Recommended Plan Names

- Launch
- Grow
- Scale

These names communicate customer growth rather than technical limits.

---

# Pricing Goals

- Increase conversions
- Encourage annual billing
- Improve renewals
- Reduce decision fatigue
- Build long-term customer loyalty

---

# Future Expansion

Future products should follow the same pricing principles to maintain consistency across the ShrotiHost ecosystem.
