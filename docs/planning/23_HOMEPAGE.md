# 23_HOMEPAGE

**Project:** ShrotiHost
**Document Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines the homepage specification for ShrotiHost.

The homepage is the most important page on the platform. It must build trust, communicate value, educate visitors, and guide them toward choosing the right hosting solution.

---

# Primary Goals

- Introduce the ShrotiHost brand
- Build trust within the first few seconds
- Explain the value proposition
- Help visitors find the right hosting plan
- Encourage conversions
- Showcase the modern identity of the platform

---

# Target Audience

- Students
- Developers
- Freelancers
- Startups
- Small Businesses

---

# User Journey

Visitor lands on homepage

↓

Understands what ShrotiHost offers

↓

Explores hosting plans or uses the Hosting Advisor

↓

Views benefits and social proof

↓

Chooses a plan

↓

Proceeds to checkout

---

# Homepage Structure

1. Announcement Bar (optional)
2. Sticky Navigation
3. Hero Section
4. Trust Indicators
5. Hosting Advisor
6. Hosting Plans
7. Why Choose ShrotiHost
8. Student Program
9. Startup Program
10. Migration Section
11. Testimonials
12. FAQ
13. Latest Articles
14. Final Call-to-Action
15. Footer

---

# Hero Section

Objectives:

- Make a strong first impression
- Clearly communicate the value proposition
- Encourage users to explore

Primary CTA:
Get Started

Secondary CTA:
Compare Plans

The hero may include a premium 3D scene following the 3D Design System.

---

# Hosting Advisor

Allow visitors to answer a few simple questions to receive a hosting recommendation.

Suggested questions:

- What are you building?
- Do you need WordPress?
- How many websites?
- Estimated traffic?
- Budget?

---

# Hosting Plans

Display Launch, Grow, and Scale.

Highlight Grow as the recommended option.

Allow switching between billing cycles.

---

# Trust Section

Include:

- LiteSpeed
- CloudLinux
- Daily Backups
- Free SSL
- Free Migration
- Imunify360

Focus on benefits rather than technical jargon.

---

# Student & Startup Programs

Provide dedicated sections with clear calls to action.

Explain eligibility and benefits.

---

# Testimonials

Display authentic customer testimonials as they become available.

Keep the layout simple and easy to scan.

---

# FAQ

Address common pre-sales questions.

Each FAQ should be expandable and SEO-friendly.

---

# Footer

Include:

- Products
- Resources
- Documentation
- Blog
- Company
- Legal
- Contact

---

# Performance Requirements

- Fast initial load
- Responsive on all devices
- Optimized images
- Lazy loading for below-the-fold content

---

# Accessibility

Homepage must support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Reduced motion preferences
- WCAG AA contrast

---

# Success Metrics

- Bounce rate reduction
- Higher conversion rate
- Increased time on page
- More plan selections
- Better Core Web Vitals

---

# Acceptance Checklist

The homepage should:

- Clearly explain the brand
- Load quickly
- Be fully responsive
- Follow the design system
- Encourage visitors to take action
