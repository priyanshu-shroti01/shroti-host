# Purpose

This document defines the identity, purpose, philosophy, and long-term direction of ShrotiHost. Every design, marketing, engineering, and product decision should align with this document.

---

# Vision

To build India's most loved hosting platform by combining premium infrastructure, exceptional customer experience, and intelligent technology at prices that remain accessible for students, developers, startups, and businesses.

ShrotiHost should become known for making hosting simple, trustworthy, and enjoyable.

---

# Mission

Empower people to launch websites, applications, APIs, portfolios, and online businesses without unnecessary complexity.

Customers should spend time building their ideas—not struggling with hosting.

---

# Brand Promise

When someone chooses ShrotiHost, they should expect:

- Honest pricing
- Fast performance
- Helpful support
- Modern technology
- Simple onboarding
- Continuous improvement

Reliable hosting with support for Node.js, Python, Laravel, WordPress, and modern workflows.

Simple infrastructure that grows with the business.

# Success Definition

Success is not measured only by revenue.

Success means:

- Customers recommend ShrotiHost.
- Students launch their first projects.
- Startups build confidently.
- Developers enjoy using the platform.
- Businesses trust us with their websites.

---

# Decision Filter

Before introducing any new feature, ask:

1. Does it solve a real customer problem?
2. Does it simplify the experience?
3. Does it improve trust?
4. Can it scale?
5. Does it strengthen the ShrotiHost brand?

If the answer is "No" to most questions, the feature should be reconsidered.
